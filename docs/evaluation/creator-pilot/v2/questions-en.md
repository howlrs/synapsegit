# Questions (Creator pilot v2)

Answer in your own words. You may look back at the pages and documents while
you answer. This is not a test with right answers. If you do not know, write
"I don't know"; that is a useful record too.

## What the record means

1. Where did the third image (AI output) come from? What did SynapseGit do
   with it?
2. What does the "File-content match check" on the review page tell you?
   What does it not tell you?
3. Suppose you want to change a decision after recording it. What happens,
   and what can you do?
4. Describe what your AI agent did today and what you did, separately.
5. Where is the backup you made in Task 6? What do you think it contains?

## Looking back

6. Which word or page was hardest to understand? How did you almost misread
   it?
7. Which steps felt like extra work for keeping a record? On a scale from 1
   (light) to 5 (heavy), how much of a burden was recording? Why?
8. When you read the record back, could you explain what you decided and
   why? Which part of the page or record helped?
9. Was anything hard about asking your AI agent, or unclear in what it
   explained?
10. If you used this in your own work, in what situations? If you would not
    use it, why not?
11. Anything else you noticed.
