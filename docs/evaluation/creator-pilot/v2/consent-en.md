# About this session and consent (Creator pilot v2)

Read this, then decide whether to take part and what may be recorded.
Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time without giving a reason.

## What the evaluation is for

SynapseGit records AI proposals and your decisions about them on your own
computer. In this session you use a version made before the official v1.0
release (a release candidate). We want to find what is hard to use, which words
are easy to misread, and any serious problem such as a lost record.
It is not a test of your skills.

## What you will do

1. Ask your AI agent to create a practice project and place a candidate (three
   images) in it.
2. Check the candidate in your browser and record Adopt, Reject, or Defer
   yourself.
3. Read the record back and make a backup.
4. Review only the necessary part of your AI agent’s command display with the
   facilitator to check who recorded the decision. Raw logs, prompts, and
   private paths are not recorded. If you cannot show the necessary part, we
   record that it could not be confirmed.
5. Answer a few questions in your own words.

The session is planned for 60 to 90 minutes. This is a guide, not a target;
you are not expected to finish quickly.

## Material

- By default you use the practice images that come with SynapseGit
  (synthetic images of a mural).
- You may choose to use your own work instead. If you do, it stays on your
  computer. The facilitator does not receive or copy your files.
- **About your AI agent:** the agent you use may send what you ask and the
  contents of files to its provider. That depends on the agent provider's
  terms, not on SynapseGit. Check this before you use your own work or private
  information. If in doubt, use the practice images.

## What is and is not recorded

The facilitator records only these observations:

- which tasks you finished; where ordinary explanation, technical substitution,
  or an undocumented workaround was needed; who did it; words you misread; and
  steps that felt like a burden
- the approximate time for each task, as an observation and not a target
- the key points of your answers
- problems found, such as defects or unclear screens
- the product name of your AI agent only if you agree to it. Otherwise we use a
  general description, such as “a terminal-capable AI agent.”

These are not recorded:

- your name, contact details, or organization
- images or titles of your work
- the text of your decision rationale, prompts to the AI, or raw agent logs

## Publication

Your record follows the choices below. Consent to publish an individual
observation is separate from consent to include your participation in a public
aggregate (the participant count and the S1--S3 conclusion). You may agree to
the latter without publishing an individual result. An individual result uses
an anonymous participant ID, such as `P1`, without linking it to your name.
Aggregate-only consent does not publish participant IDs, individual results,
or private record locations.

If a product issue needs to be published, the evaluation team reproduces it
independently with synthetic material. That issue contains no participant ID,
session, or participant observation and does not expand publication of your
individual observations.

The facilitator and evaluation team may inspect private records within the
scope you permit. A public summary contains only consented observations and
protocol metadata, such as the version used. It excludes your work, information
that identifies you, decision rationale, prompts, and raw logs.

## If you want to stop

You can stop at any time. On request, records that are not yet published are
deleted. Published summaries remain, without information that identifies you.
